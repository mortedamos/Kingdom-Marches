Create a single square game terrain tile image, NOT a grid, NOT
multiple panels — one single flat square image only.

Game asset: Coast (shallow water) terrain tile, standard variant, frame
1 of a 2-frame gentle shimmer idle animation loop. Coast should read as
a lighter, shallower cousin of the game's deep ocean water — the same
blue color family, just brighter and more turquoise — since coast and
ocean tiles need to visually blend into one continuous body of water
when placed next to each other on the map. This is calmer than the
ocean tile: a gentle sunlit shimmer, not rolling waves.

Style: this is a TERRAIN tile, not a character or object — it must
recede visually behind game units, which use bold black outlines and
sharp cel-shaded flat colors. The water itself uses soft, smoothly
blended color gradients with no hard black outlines. This tile is also
rendered very small on screen, so it must read as one or two bold,
simple shapes — not a detailed illustration of many small ripples.

Subject: shallow coastal water, viewed from directly above (top-down).
The base fill is this flat solid color: hex #3D80A0 (a brighter,
shallower turquoise-blue than deep ocean, but clearly the same blue
family). On top of that base fill, add 2 soft, round-ish glowing
highlight patches (like sunlight glinting off gently shifting shallow
water), each a soft radial gradient of a pale bright cyan (hex
#A8E0EA) that fades smoothly outward into the base color with NO hard
edge and NO outline of any kind — just a soft glow. Position them
loosely scattered, not in a tight cluster, and not touching each other.
Keep it simple and calm — do not add wave-crest lines, foam, or busy
ripple texture; this tile should read as noticeably calmer/gentler than
a rough sea.

Framing: top-down flattened tile view (camera looking straight down,
NOT an elevated 3/4 angle), square 1:1 composition. The base color fill
extends completely edge-to-edge across the entire image with zero
padding or margin.

CRITICAL: there is no black outline, border, frame, or line drawn
around the edge of the image, and there are no corner brackets, crop
marks, tick marks, or any small graphic mark of any kind in any of the
four corners or along any edge — completely plain and unmarked at the
tile boundary. There is also no vignette or corner darkening — overall
brightness stays even corner to corner. This is a single seamless
texture tile meant to butt up directly against identical neighboring
tiles with zero visible seam, so nothing in this image may mark or
outline its own boundary in any way.

Do not include any text, labels, numbers, watermark, logo, UI elements,
or border anywhere in the image. Overall image should be a square 1:1
aspect ratio.
