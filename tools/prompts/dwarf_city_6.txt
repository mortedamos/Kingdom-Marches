Game asset: Dwarf City, tier 6 (race: Dwarf, category: city tier).

A single game asset in a cel-shaded outline style: thick, clean black
outlines around the silhouette and major interior linework, sharp
details, cel-shaded flat colors with a limited palette (2-3 hard-edged
tonal steps per surface -- base, shadow, occasional highlight -- no soft
airbrushed gradients or blurry ambient occlusion). Clear legibility when
zoomed out to a small size. Muted, desaturated, earthy fantasy palette
(olive greens, weathered browns, stone grays, faded ochres) -- no
saturated cartoon colors. Dark-fantasy mood, weathered and lived-in, not
cute or whimsical. Single consistent light direction from the upper left,
implied by the shadow/highlight color steps rather than rendered soft
light.

This asset must stay legible when shrunk down to a very small size, so:
use strong contrast between neighboring color regions (a bright/light
element against a dark one, not two similar mid-tones touching); hold
identifying details out and away from each other rather than overlapping,
so key shapes don't merge together; and keep ornamental detail minimal --
favor a small number of bold, simple shapes over fine texture like
individual mortar lines that won't survive downscaling anyway.

Subject: a grand dwarven stronghold -- the widest and most massive tier
yet, a sprawling fortified complex of many joined stone halls, thick
double-course walls, six or more squat corner and midwall towers (still
low and blocky, battlement-capped, not tall spires), a large forge
building with three stone chimneys. CRITICAL: this is a peaceful,
prosperous stronghold going about its business, NOT a building under
attack or on fire -- each chimney has only a SMALL, CONTAINED warm
orange glow right at its very top opening (like light escaping a
chimney flue) plus a thin wisp of gray smoke drifting up, comparable to
a lit window at dusk. Do not draw large flames, fire bursts, or any
flame shape rising above or outside the chimney's stonework -- if it
could be mistaken for the building burning down, it's wrong. A truly
grand reinforced gate flanked by two carved stone guardian pillars,
heavy iron banding and rivets throughout, and a wide, elaborate arch of
carved rune-work above the gate -- the most ornate rune-work in the
whole sequence. This is freestanding stone construction, NOT built into
or against a cliff/mountain face -- an immense fortress standing on open
ground by sheer mass and craftsmanship alone. Several tan/brown
(#9a7b56) banners along the wall and towers as the only accent-color
elements.

Framing: elevated isometric diorama angle (~30-40 degree downward tilt),
the full settlement visible as a self-contained island silhouette (not a
filled square background), portrait 1:2 (width:height) composition. This
is the sixth and final tier in a 6-tier growth sequence that stays LOW
AND WIDE throughout (growth reads as increasing mass and fortification,
not height -- this is a deliberate contrast to other races whose tiers

clearly, deliberately SHORTER than a tall spire-style city tier from
another race, impressive through sheer width and density of stonework
rather than height. Ground level sits at the very bottom edge of the
frame. The ground itself must read as an irregular, organic footprint --
uneven natural terrain, a rough dirt clearing -- NEVER a crisp geometric
diamond, rhombus, or flat rotated square platform (that would read as a
separate isometric tile, not an object sitting on the game's actual flat
top-down square grid).

Background: a single flat, solid, saturated magenta (#FF00FF) background,
completely uniform, no gradient, no shadow cast onto it, no vignette --
this will be chroma-keyed out, so nothing in the subject itself should be
magenta or near-magenta.

Race material language: roughly-hewn gray stone blocks as the dominant
material, dark iron banding/rivets as a recurring metal accent
throughout, three glowing forge chimneys, elaborate carved rune-work at
the gate. The tan/brown (#9a7b56) accent color may only appear as the
small banners described above -- never as the dominant hue of stone or
metal.

Do not include any text, watermark, logo, UI elements, or border in the
