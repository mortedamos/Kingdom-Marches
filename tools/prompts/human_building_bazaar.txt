Game asset: Bazaar (race: Human, category: building).

A single game asset in a cel-shaded outline style: thick, clean black
outlines around the silhouette and major interior linework, sharp
details, cel-shaded flat colors with a limited palette (2-3 hard-edged
tonal steps per surface -- base, shadow, occasional highlight -- no soft
airbrushed gradients or blurry ambient occlusion). Clear legibility when
zoomed out to a small size. Dark-fantasy mood overall, but this race's
stonework is a deliberate exception to the usual muted-earthy palette:
genuinely pale, bright white-gray dressed stone, clearly lighter than
weathered wood or green foliage. Single consistent light direction from
the upper left, implied by the shadow/highlight color steps rather than
rendered soft light.

This asset must stay legible when shrunk down to a very small size, so:
use strong contrast between neighboring color regions; keep ornamental
detail minimal -- favor a small number of bold, simple shapes over fine
texture that won't survive downscaling anyway.

Subject: a bustling human bazaar -- a low, open-fronted pale white-gray
stone building with a colorful striped awning (purple #8e44ad and
cream) shading a row of market stalls beneath it, a few crates, sacks,
and baskets of goods visible along the front, a hanging wooden sign.
Multiple small purple (#8e44ad) pennants along the awning's edge. This
is a single dedicated STRUCTURE on its own tile, not a settlement.

Framing: elevated isometric diorama angle (~30-40 degree downward tilt),
the structure visible as a self-contained island silhouette, portrait
composition, content anchored to the bottom of the frame -- ground level
sits at the very bottom edge. Low and wide -- occupying roughly the
bottom 35-40% of the frame, clearly shorter than a city tier. The ground
itself must read as an irregular, organic footprint -- uneven natural
terrain -- NEVER a crisp geometric diamond, rhombus, or flat rotated
square platform (that would read as a separate isometric tile, not an
object sitting on the game's actual flat top-down square grid).

Background: a single flat, solid, saturated magenta (#FF00FF) background,
completely uniform, no gradient, no shadow cast onto it, no vignette --
this will be chroma-keyed out, so nothing in the subject itself should be
magenta or near-magenta.

Race material language: pale dressed white-gray stone as the dominant
material, a purple-and-cream striped awning as trade dressing. The
purple (#8e44ad) accent color appears on the awning stripes and small
pennants described above -- never as the dominant hue of the stone
itself.

image.
