Game asset: Orc City, tier 1 (race: Orc, category: city tier).

A single game asset in a cel-shaded outline style: thick, clean black
outlines around the silhouette and major interior linework, sharp
details, cel-shaded flat colors with a limited palette (2-3 hard-edged
tonal steps per surface -- base, shadow, occasional highlight -- no soft
airbrushed gradients or blurry ambient occlusion). Clear legibility when
zoomed out to a small size. Muted, desaturated, earthy fantasy palette
(olive greens, weathered browns, stone grays, faded ochres) -- no
saturated cartoon colors. Dark-fantasy mood, weathered and lived-in, not
cute or whimsical. Single consistent light direction from the upper left,
implied by the shadow/highlight color steps rather than rendered soft
light.

This asset must stay legible when shrunk down to a very small size, so:
use strong contrast between neighboring color regions; hold identifying
details out and away from each other rather than overlapping; and keep
ornamental detail minimal -- favor a small number of bold, simple shapes
over fine texture that won't survive downscaling anyway. No population
figures scattered around -- this is an unpopulated environmental
diorama, not a crowd scene.

CRITICAL legibility constraint for this race specifically: a previous
Orc city art set crammed in many small huts, towers, banners, and
crowds of tiny figures all at once, and it read as an indistinct brown
blur at actual small in-game render size -- all visual impact was lost
to clutter. Do NOT repeat that mistake. Use the FEWEST possible number
of distinct structures/shapes to convey this tier, each one large and
bold rather than many small ones, with real empty space between
elements so the silhouette stays simple and immediately readable even
tiny. When in doubt, cut a detail rather than add one.

Subject: the very first tiny orc camp -- a single crude tent of
weathered hide and lashed branches, a couple of rough-hewn log posts
driven into the ground beside it (the beginning of a perimeter, not a
full fence yet), a small pile of scavenged firewood. One small red
(#7a2e2e) banner with a crude skull motif hangs from one of the posts
as the only accent-color element.

Framing: elevated isometric diorama angle (~30-40 degree downward tilt),
the full settlement visible as a self-contained island silhouette (not a
filled square background), portrait 1:2 (width:height) composition. This
is the smallest tier in a 6-tier growth sequence that stays LOW AND WIDE
throughout (Orc growth reads as an increasingly large and fortified
tribal camp, not height) -- small and sparse, occupying roughly the
bottom 18% of the frame, leaving the upper portion empty/transparent.
Ground level sits at the very bottom edge of the frame. The ground
itself must read as an irregular, organic footprint -- uneven dirt and
scrub -- NEVER a crisp geometric diamond, rhombus, or flat rotated
square platform (that would read as a separate isometric tile, not an
object sitting on the game's actual flat top-down square grid). The ground's edge must also read as a SOFT, ORGANIC blend into the surrounding terrain -- an irregular, slightly ragged silhouette with no straight or angular facet edges, never a hard polygonal outline (like a rock mesa or gemstone cut) that would read as a separate rigid platform.

Background: a single flat, solid, saturated magenta (#FF00FF) background,
completely uniform, no gradient, no shadow cast onto it, no vignette --
this will be chroma-keyed out, so nothing in the subject itself should be
magenta or near-magenta.

Race material language: weathered hide and lashed branches as the tent
material, rough-hewn log posts, a crude skull motif. The red (#7a2e2e)
accent color may only appear as the small banner described above --
never as the dominant hue of hide or wood.

Do not include any text, watermark, logo, UI elements, or border in the
image.
