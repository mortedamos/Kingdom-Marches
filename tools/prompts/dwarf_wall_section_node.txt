Game asset: Wall Segment, junction/watchtower bastion (race: Dwarf, category: wall).

A single game asset in a cel-shaded outline style: thick, clean black
outlines around the silhouette and major interior linework, sharp
details, cel-shaded flat colors with a limited palette (2-3 hard-edged
tonal steps per surface -- base, shadow, occasional highlight -- no soft
airbrushed gradients or blurry ambient occlusion). Clear legibility when
zoomed out to a small size. Muted, desaturated, earthy fantasy palette
(olive greens, weathered browns, stone grays, faded ochres) -- no
saturated cartoon colors. Dark-fantasy mood, weathered and lived-in, not
cute or whimsical. Single consistent light direction from the upper left,
implied by the shadow/highlight color steps rather than rendered soft

This asset must stay legible when shrunk down to a very small size, so:
use strong contrast between neighboring color regions; keep ornamental
detail minimal -- favor a small number of bold, simple shapes over fine
texture that won't survive downscaling anyway.

Subject: a solid, blocky dwarven watchtower bastion, used wherever the
wall bends, joins another stretch of wall from any direction, or stands
alone -- built from the same massive roughly-hewn gray stone blocks as
the straight wall segments. CRITICAL shape: this is a SQUARE, BLOCKY
structure that fills the tile edge-to-edge on ALL FOUR SIDES -- the
stonework's outer footprint reaches all the way to the left, right,
top, AND bottom edges of the frame simultaneously (not a smaller
central tower with gaps at the corners -- the base of the bastion is a
solid filled square matching the frame). Because the base is a solid
square block, a straight wall segment can plug into it seamlessly from
ANY of the four cardinal directions -- there is no tapering the way a
round tower would have, the stonework is simply at full height along
its entire edge. Rising from the center of this square base is a
slightly taller, more heavily fortified square tower section with
simple stone battlements along its top edge, and a horizontal band of
dark iron plating with rivets wrapping around it partway up. No banner,
no living/organic elements -- pure stone and iron, the most heavily
fortified-looking piece in the Dwarf wall set.

Framing: SQUARE 1:1 composition (equal width and height), matching one
terrain tile -- same convention as this game's road and river tile art,
not the portrait canvas used for standalone buildings, and the SAME
square aspect ratio as the straight horizontal/vertical wall tiles, so
any edge lines up with them exactly. The base fills the frame edge to
edge as described; the central tower section may rise higher within
this square frame than the straight segments' wall height, but must
still read as clearly shorter than an ordinary building's tallest form,
let alone a city spire.

Background: a single flat, solid, saturated magenta (#FF00FF)
background, completely uniform, no gradient, no shadow cast onto it, no
vignette -- this will be chroma-keyed out, so nothing in the subject
itself should be magenta or near-magenta. Magenta should NOT show at
any of the four edges -- the stone base must reach every edge fully
(unlike a round tower, there are no empty corners here; the whole
square silhouette is stone).

Race material language: massive roughly-hewn gray stone blocks as the
dominant material, dark iron plating with rivets as a recurring metal
accent, simple stone battlements as the distinguishing detail for this
"tallest" wall variant.
