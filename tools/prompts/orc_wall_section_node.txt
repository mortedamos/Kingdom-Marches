Game asset: Wall Segment, junction/totem cluster (race: Orc, category: wall).

A single game asset in a cel-shaded outline style: thick, clean black
outlines around the silhouette and major interior linework, sharp
details, cel-shaded flat colors with a limited palette (2-3 hard-edged
tonal steps per surface -- base, shadow, occasional highlight -- no soft
airbrushed gradients or blurry ambient occlusion). Clear legibility when
zoomed out to a small size. Muted, desaturated, earthy fantasy palette
(olive greens, weathered browns, stone grays, faded ochres) -- no
saturated cartoon colors. Dark-fantasy mood, weathered and lived-in, not
cute or whimsical. Single consistent light direction from the upper left,
implied by the shadow/highlight color steps rather than rendered soft
light.

This asset must stay legible when shrunk down to a very small size, so:
use strong contrast between neighboring color regions; keep ornamental
detail minimal -- favor a small number of bold, simple shapes over fine
texture that won't survive downscaling anyway.

Subject: an orc tribal totem-junction, used wherever the wall bends,
joins another stretch of wall from any direction, or stands alone. This
has TWO layered parts, and the distinction between them matters:

1. THE CONNECTING BASE (must be geometrically reliable): a solid ring of
lashed sharpened logs identical in height and thickness to the straight
wall segments, wrapping all the way around the tile so it reaches all
FOUR sides of the square frame at its connecting band-height -- this
base must present the SAME log-palisade band, at the SAME vertical
position, on every side, so a straight wall segment placed on any
neighboring tile connects into it cleanly no matter which direction it
approaches from. Think of this base as circular/octagonal, not a
plus/cross shape with thin arms -- one continuous ring of logs, full
band-width all the way around.

CRITICAL sizing: the ring must be drawn LARGE, filling nearly the ENTIRE
canvas with only minimal padding -- do NOT draw a smaller composition
centered with lots of empty margin around it the way a standalone
building would be framed. The ring's log band must reach within a few
pixels of all four frame edges, the same tight-to-the-edge framing the
straight horizontal/vertical wall tiles use. A small centered
composition floating in a mostly-empty square frame is WRONG for this
asset -- it must look like a tight crop of a wall ring that continues
believably past all four edges, not an object sitting in open space.

2. THE TRIBAL ORNAMENT (can be as wild and irregular as fits the tribe):
rising from the CENTER of that base, an irregular cluster of totem
poles, sharpened stakes, and mounted skulls/bones of varying heights
and angles -- chaotic, asymmetric, unmistakably tribal, taller and
bolder than anything on the straight segments. This cluster sits well
inside the base ring, not touching or extending past the frame's edges
at all -- all of its visual chaos stays in the middle of the tile,
never interfering with the clean connecting ring beneath it. One red
(#7a2e2e) banner or trophy-hide hangs from the tallest totem pole as
the only accent-color element.

Framing: SQUARE 1:1 composition (equal width and height), matching one
terrain tile -- same convention as this game's road and river tile art,
not the portrait canvas used for standalone buildings, and the SAME
square aspect ratio as the straight horizontal/vertical wall tiles, so
the connecting base ring lines up with them exactly on every side. The
totem cluster may rise higher within this square frame than the
straight segments' log tops do, but must still read as clearly shorter
than an ordinary building's tallest form, let alone a city spire.

Background: a single flat, solid, saturated magenta (#FF00FF)
background, completely uniform, no gradient, no shadow cast onto it, no
vignette -- this will be chroma-keyed out, so nothing in the subject
itself should be magenta or near-magenta. Magenta should fill the four
corner areas outside the connecting ring.

Race material language: roughly-sharpened wooden logs with crude rope
lashings for the base ring, weathered totem poles and bone/skull trophies
for the central cluster. The red (#7a2e2e) accent color may only appear
as the small banner/hide described above -- never as the dominant hue
of wood or bone.

Do not include any text, watermark, logo, UI elements, or border in the
image.
