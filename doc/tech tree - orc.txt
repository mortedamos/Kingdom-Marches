ORC TECH TREE (reviewed against js/data/techs.js, 2026-08-10)

Every civ also has 5 shared Level 0 techs, auto-completed for free at civ
creation (never actually researched): Pioneer, Distant Horizons (Scout),
Distant Shores (Galley), Hunt Game, Farm Soil. Not listed below since
they're identical for every race.

COST MODEL: every tech's real cost is purely layer-based (doubling each
layer: L1=20 total, L2=40, L3=80, L4=160, L5=320), split across
harvest/coin/lore by column -- Civic leans Harvest (50/20/30), Building
leans Coin (10/60/30), Military splits Coin/Lore evenly (20/40/40), Mystic
leans Lore hardest of all (10/30/60). The numbers below are the REAL costs
a player pays, not the hand-authored `cost`/`costBreakdown` fields in
techs.js (inert leftover data). A city needs at least as many cities as a
tech's layer number to research it.

FOUR COLUMNS, not three (2026-08-10, user-directed): Mystic split off from
Military -- the Bog Witch and its own curse tech moved there, since a
caster's kit doesn't fit alongside straight combat techs. Orc's Mystic
column is deliberately thin (just these two) -- Orc is a raiding/combat
race first, with only one real caster unit, unlike Human/Elf/Dwarf's
deeper caster kits.

FORCED MARCH / WASTELAND RIDERS now apply to every Orc unit (2026-08-10,
user-directed), not just Raiders/Wolf Riders/Ogres -- both used to carry a
`units:` allowlist restricting the terrain-movement discount to those
three; that restriction is gone.

UNIT STATS are no longer written into descriptions below (2026-08-10) --
the tech tree UI now shows each unlocked unit's live stats (Atk/Def/Mov/
Vis/Range/Siege/First Strike/Double Strike/Flying/Carry as applicable)
computed fresh from units.js at render time, folding in whatever that civ
has already researched, so it can never drift out of sync the way a
hand-typed number in a description can.

CORRECTIONS from the previous version of this file: three whole techs
existed in code but were missing from this doc -- Miscreant (L1 Military,
Goblin Miscreant unit), Spikes! and Bigger Spikes! (L2/L3 Building, wall/
city counterattack) -- all added below. Violent Momentum also grants 10%
First Strike and 10% Double Strike, not just +2 movement. Swift Hunters
grants 2% First Strike, not 15%. Tip of the Spear grants 2.5% First
Strike, not 25%. War Camp's -20% cost/build-time/upkeep, Burn It All
Down's Goblin Miscreant exception, and Draconic Mastery's 45% compounding
rarity premium were all independently verified against buildings.js/ai.js
and are correct as previously written.


--- CIVIC ---

ORC - CIVIC - L1: Marsh Paths - Reduces the movement cost of Swamp by 0.5. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L1: Forced March - Reduces the movement cost of Plains by 0.5. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L1: Violent Momentum - +2 movement, 10% First Strike, and 10% Double Strike for a unit that killed an enemy unit the previous turn. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L2: Wetland Harvest - +0.5 harvest from Swamp. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L2: Spoils of War (requires War Camp) - When an Orc unit kills an enemy unit, gain +3 coin and +4 lore. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L3: Pillage and Loot (requires Spoils of War) - An Orc unit standing within an enemy city's radius has a 2-tile (Chebyshev) radius around itself which removes any filled-in enemy influence from those tiles -- cutting off their yield immediately and fully stripping the tile after 3 turns of sustained suppression -- and generates +1 harvest, +1 coin, and +1 lore for EACH tile where influence was actually suppressed this turn. Lasts until the unit moves to a new tile (the effect moves with it), leaves the enemy city's radius, or is killed. Does not stack with other Orc units on the same tile. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L4: The Old Ways - +0.5 lore from Swamp. [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L4: Honor the Dead - When an Orc unit dies, gain +5 lore. Orc units have a 50% chance to resist being raised from the dead (if an Orc unit is slain by an Undead unit, only a 50% chance the Undead civ turns it into a zombie under its control). [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

ORC - CIVIC - L5: Cultural Influence (requires every other Orc tech already researched) - Tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately, skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait. Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]


--- BUILDING ---

ORC - BUILDING - L2: War Camp - Unlocks the War Camp building: Orc unit cost is reduced by 20% across all resources (not just coin), build time is reduced by the same 20%, and ongoing per-turn upkeep for the whole army is also reduced by 20%. [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

ORC - BUILDING - L2: Spikes! - Walls and cities can counterattack (not other buildings), with an attack rating of 1. [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

ORC - BUILDING - L3: Butchery - Unlocks the Butchery building (+10% harvest, any time harvest is gained). [8 harvest, 48 coin, 24 lore]

ORC - BUILDING - L3: Bigger Spikes! (requires Spikes!) - Walls and cities can counterattack (not other buildings), with an attack rating of 2 -- replaces (does not stack with) Spikes!'s attack rating. [8 harvest, 48 coin, 24 lore]

ORC - BUILDING - L4: Dragon Den - Unlocks the Dragon Den building (+10% coin, any time coin is gained). [16 harvest, 96 coin, 48 lore]

ORC - BUILDING - L5: Ancestral Dolmen (requires The Old Ways) - Unlocks the Ancestral Dolmen building (+10% lore, any time lore is gained). [32 harvest, 192 coin, 96 lore]


--- MILITARY ---

ORC - MILITARY - L1: Raiders - Unlocks the Raider, the orcs' basic melee fighter. Granted free at civ creation, same as every other race's basic melee unit. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L1: Miscreant - Unlocks the Goblin Miscreant, a fast, cheap, disposable gap-filler unit rather than a real fighter -- cheaper to build and maintain than its stats alone suggest. Building one actually produces two: the second spawns on a random adjacent tile next to the city. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L1: Dire Wolf - Unlocks the Dire Wolf: a fast, savage hunter-beast. Regardless of fog of war, a Dire Wolf always knows the way to the nearest enemy unit on its own landmass and relentlessly closes the distance, attacking anything it catches along the way -- its preferred action, ahead of every other Orc strategy. Gains +2 movement while actively hunting (2026-08-10). Player-facing as of 2026-08-10: available as a "Hunt for Prey" ring action, not AI-only. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L2: Wolf Riders (requires Dire Wolf) - Unlocks the Wolf Rider, a fast-moving mounted raider. Also reduces the movement cost of Forest by 0.5 for Wolf Riders only. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L2: Impaler Rites - Unlocks the Impaler, a sturdy defender built to hold ground rather than strike hard. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L2: Swift Hunters (requires Wolf Riders) - Wolf Rider gains 2% First Strike and increased attack (+2) and movement (+1). [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Tip of the Spear (requires Impaler Rites) - Impaler gains 2.5% First Strike. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Battering Ram - Unlocks the Battering Ram, a slow-moving siege engine built to break down walls and structures. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Ogre - Unlocks the Ogre, a hard-hitting brute that can also help break down walls and structures. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Burn It All Down - Ranged attacks (not adjacent melee) from a Scout or a Dragon set their target ablaze; a Goblin Miscreant's melee attacks also ignite the target (it has no ranged option, so the adjacency restriction doesn't apply to it). The Burning condition deals 1 damage at the start of the burning unit's turn for 3 turns, unless it's currently on Coast, Ocean, or a river tile. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Wasteland Riders (requires Forced March) - Reduces the movement cost of Desert by 0.5. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L3: Hound and Hunter (requires Wolf Riders and Dire Wolf) - When a Wolf Rider dies, 50% chance a Raider or a Dire Wolf (50/50 between the two) spawns in its place. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L4: Siege Tactics (requires Ogre and Battering Ram) - If an Orc unit already has the Siege property, it increases by 20 percentage points; otherwise the unit gains Siege 20%. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L5: Draconic Mastery (requires Dragon Den) - Unlocks the Dragon, a flying terror and the single most powerful unit in the game -- but each Dragon a civ already owns makes the next one substantially more expensive and slower to build, so fielding many at once is deliberately punishing. [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]

ORC - MILITARY - L5: Dragon Riders (requires Draconic Mastery) - Dragon gains the Carry property (can only carry one other Orc unit, not another Dragon). If the Dragon dies while carrying a unit, that unit spawns into the space the Dragon formerly occupied. A carried unit cannot act except to disembark. [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]


--- MYSTIC ---
(split out of Military, 2026-08-10 -- see the note at the top of this file)

ORC - MYSTIC - L2: Bog Witch - Unlocks the Bog Witch. Any unit that kills a Bog Witch loses 50% attack and 50% movement for 5 turns -- a death-curse for whoever finishes her off. [4 harvest, 12 coin, 24 lore]

ORC - MYSTIC - L3: Malefic Malediction (requires Bog Witch) - Any unit damaged by a Bog Witch loses 50% attack and 50% movement for 5 turns. Stacks alongside the Bog Witch's own death-curse. [8 harvest, 24 coin, 48 lore]

ORC - MYSTIC - L3: Bog Spirit (requires Bog Witch) - The Bog Witch may instantly summon a Wisp, a small flying spirit of the swamp, into any swamp tile the Orc kingdom has ever explored (doesn't need to be currently visible) -- no build delay, it appears immediately. The Wisp flies at normal flying speed but is permanently confined to swamp terrain -- it can never cross onto any other tile type. It can hide, has no upkeep, and the Orc kingdom may field at most one Wisp per living Bog Witch; if a Bog Witch dies and that leaves too many Wisps, one must be disbanded. [4 harvest, 12 coin, 24 lore]
