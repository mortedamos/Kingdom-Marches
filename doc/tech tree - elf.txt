ELF - CIVIC - L4: "Wind From Distant Treetops" (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves") - All forest tiles anywhere on the map are always revealed (no fog of war in those tiles, all forest tiles are always visible to elf player/AI).
ELF - CIVIC - L5: "Vision Beyond Sight" (requires "Air Beneath, Eyes Above") - All elf units gain +1 vision.
ELF - CIVIC - L5: "Cultural Influence" (requires every other Elf tech already researched) - True tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately (skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait). Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. A late-game sink for otherwise-idle surplus stockpile.

ELF - BUILDING - L2: "Treetop Snipers" (requires "Aelderwatch" AND "Hunter's Soul") - If an enemy unit is within range, there is a 50% chance each turn that an elf wall will attack that enemy unit. The wall has range 2, attack 1.
ELF - BUILDING - L3: "Silverleaf Atelier" (requires "Architecture of Light and Air" AND "Gems of Starlight") - unlocks the Silverleaf Atelier building, which grants +10% coin and +10% lore to its city.
ELF - BUILDING - L4: "Altar of Ages" (requires "Druidism" AND "Reverie of Sunset") - unlocks the Altar of Ages building, granting all elf units created in this city gain an extra 25% xp. 
ELF - BUILDING - L5: "Wellspring Grove" - (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves" AND "Whispering Waters") All elf units and walls and buildings within this city's radius heal 5% health per turn (minimum 1 HP), regardless of if they are resting or not.



ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Watching, Hunting" - unlocks the Ranger unit. Range 2, first strike. Granted free at civ creation, same as every other race's basic unit.
ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Wind and Flashing Steel" - unlocks the Blade Dancer unit. Melee, first strike.
ELF - MILITARY - L2: "Whirlwind Strike" (requires "Wind and Flashing Steel") - As a normal action, the Blade Dancer attacks every enemy unit within 1 tile of itself, each hit dealing 75% damage (as if attacking normally). Each target may counterattack, at 37.5% effectiveness. Floating text names the sweep, and its radius briefly highlights on the map.
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Blade Storm" (requires "Whirlwind Strike") - Does not replace Whirlwind Strike -- both stay independently usable, the AI picks whichever suits the current cluster of targets. As a normal action, the Blade Dancer attacks every enemy unit within 2 tiles of itself, each hit dealing 50% damage (as if attacking normally). Only targets adjacent to the Blade Dancer may counterattack, at 25% effectiveness. Floating text names the sweep, and its radius briefly highlights on the map.
ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Shadowed Hush, Unseen" - Every elf unit may spend its whole turn to become Hidden for 3 turns (voluntarily cancellable early). Cannot activate with an enemy unit on an adjacent tile. Unit becomes unhidden when it attacks. Whenever a Hidden unit is revealed for any reason, it is forced visible for at least 1 turn before it can go Hidden again. 
ELF - MILITARY - L2: Strike from the Shadows (requires "Shadowed Hush, Unseen") - A Hidden elf unit gains +50% bonus to attack score. Attacking uses this bonus, then ends Hidden as normal. Implemented AI: weighs an isolated sneak-ambush against a full multi-unit assault based on militarism; goes Hidden defensively when it would probably lose a straight fight, and offensively can hold its strongest nearby unit visible as bait while weaker allies go Hidden nearby to spring the ambush once this tech makes it worth the setup. Different from halfellow - elf ranger, because of their ranged attacks, may make use of this tactic even against stronger enemies or while a single unit without allies nearby - hide, attack at range with the attack bonus, then hide again. 
ELF - MILITARY - L2: Druidism (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves" AND "Whispering Waters") - unlocks the druid unit (magic unit, like human wizard, utility unit). range 2. may found a city (in addition to the Pioneer)
ELF - MILITARY - L2: "Air Beneath, Eyes Above" (requires "Druidism") - The druid may summon a Raptor to assist elven allies. Like a city, the druid "builds" the unit, spending resources and several rounds of time to do so. While building this unit, the druid cannot move or take other actions (but it can build this unit while hidden, if it was already hidden before it started). The raptor unit has attack 1, defense 0, flight, first strike 2%, very high movement and very high vision. Its purpose in game is to explore and scout, and should priortize doing so. Cities cannot build Raptor units.
ELF - MILITARY - L2: "First Frost of Autumn" - Elf unit attacks have a 10% chance to inflict the Frozen condition.
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Hunter's Soul" (requires "Watching, Hunting") - Rangers gain +1 range and +1 attack. 
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Nature's Grace" (requires "Druidism" AND "Nature Provides") - The druid unit may use this action (costs its whole turn, no exhaustion afterward) to restore between 10% to 30% (random) health to an ally unit within its own range (not just adjacent) -- same clear-shot-through-mountains requirement a ranged attack uses beyond adjacency. A Druid mounted on a Shadowsteed doesn't lose access to this: the Shadowsteed itself may cast it instead, at the same range the Druid would have.
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Steely Eyed" - All elf units gain +1 vision. 
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Sudden Doom" (requires "Strike from the Shadows") - Replacing Strike from Shadows (one effect, not two stacking bonuses), the bonus to attack score while hidden increases to +100% instead of +50% and the unit also gains first strike +10%. Both of these bonuses end when the unit is no longer hidden.
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Silverlight Steel" (requires "Silverleaf Atelier") - Blade Dancers gain +1 attack and +1% first strike.
ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Roots of the World" (requires "Druidism") - A druid unit may instantly move itself, or an adjacent ally, to any unoccupied tile the civ has ever explored (not just currently visible). This costs the druid's entire turn (no move/attack after), and it (the druid, not a relocated ally) cannot act again until healed to 100% HP. Implemented AI: uses this defensively (a badly hurt druid blinks itself to the safest known tile, or blinks a badly hurt ADJACENT ally to safety instead), and to expand: a druid civ looking for a place to build a city may consider far-off forest tiles, teleport the druid there, then found a new city. Overseas invasion ferry: once the civ knows of an enemy city/wall/building on a landmass other than the druid's own, druids become much more focused on teleporting Rangers, Blade Dancers, and Awakened Oaks to those places (checked ahead of healing/summoning/expansion) -- the Elf answer to the galley bottleneck, and the only way an Awakened Oak (movement 1) ever crosses water. Ferried units arrive at a staging ground 3-4 tiles from the target (clustered together, preferring Forest), go Hidden if researched, and WAIT -- accumulating as more units are ferried in -- until the gathered force has enough combined power to overcome the visible defenders (or no defenders remain, or it has waited long enough), at which point the whole force springs its ambush attack at once, any still-Hidden members attacking out of stealth with the full Strike from the Shadows bonus.
ELF - MILITARY - L4: "Quick as a Shadow" (requires "Strike from the Shadows") - A hidden elf unit can move at full speed, unlike most hidden units. Additionally, elf units (other than the Druid, whose turn is better spent on its own kit) now prefer to be Hidden as their default resting state, going hidden proactively even with no enemy in sight, rather than only reacting to a nearby threat -- a unit already engaged in combat (adjacent to an enemy) is unaffected and fights normally.
ELF - MILITARY - L4: "Sanctuary under Green Boughs" - All elves gain +20% defense when in forest. 
ELF - MILITARY - L5: "Shadowsteed" (requires "Air Beneath, Eyes Above" AND "Quick as a Shadow") - The druid may summon a flying horse made of shadow to assist elven allies. Like a city, the druid "builds" the unit, spending resources and several rounds of time to do so. While building this unit, the druid cannot move or take other actions (but it can build this unit while hidden, if it was already hidden before it started). The "shadow steed" is its own unit with flying, carry, first strike 5%, attack 1, defense 1. It cannot carry an Awakened Oak, Raptor, or Galley. When it carries another unit, it gains the range, attack, defense, siege, and first strike of that unit (keeping its own first strike if higher). Then it gains 3 attack, 2 defense, and +2% first strike while carrying a unit. AI strategy: a Shadowsteed strongly prefers a Ranger as its rider -- it takes an adjacent Ranger immediately, but otherwise won't settle for an adjacent Druid/Blade Dancer as long as an uncarried Ranger exists anywhere within its seek range, holding out (and closing in on that Ranger instead) rather than reflexively mounting whichever ally -- often its own summoning Druid -- happens to be standing next to it the instant it's born. Only mounts a Druid/Blade Dancer once no Ranger is available anywhere within reach. Cities cannot build Shadow Steed units. Whenever the Shadowsteed earns combat XP, its carried rider earns the exact same amount too.
ELF - MILITARY - L5: "The Living Forest" (requires "Druidism" AND "Sanctuary under Green Boughs") - unlocks the Awakened Oak unit, a living, walking tree. (massive, rare, very tough, high attack and defense, high siege, neverExplores).
ELF - MILITARY - L5: "Upon the Wind" (requires "Hunter's Soul" AND "Vision Beyond Sight") - All Ranger and Blade Dancer units gain +2 attack. Ranger units gain +1 range while being carried (by a Shadowsteed).
