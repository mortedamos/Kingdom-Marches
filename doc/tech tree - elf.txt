ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Shadowed Hush, Unseen" - Every elf unit may spend its whole turn to become Hidden for 3 turns (voluntarily cancellable early). Cannot activate with an enemy unit on an adjacent tile. Unit becomes unhidden when it attacks. Whenever a Hidden unit is revealed for any reason, it is forced visible for at least 1 turn before it can go Hidden again. 
ELF - MILITARY - L2: Strike from the Shadows (requires "Shadowed Hush, Unseen") - A Hidden elf unit gains +50% bonus to attack score. Attacking uses this bonus, then ends Hidden as normal. Implemented AI: weighs an isolated sneak-ambush against a full multi-unit assault based on militarism; goes Hidden defensively when it would probably lose a straight fight, and offensively can hold its strongest nearby unit visible as bait while weaker allies go Hidden nearby to spring the ambush once this tech makes it worth the setup. Different from halfellow - elf ranger, because of their ranged attacks, may make use of this tactic even against stronger enemies or while a single unit without allies nearby - hide, attack at range with the attack bonus, then hide again.
