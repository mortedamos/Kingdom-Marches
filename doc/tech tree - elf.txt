ELF TECH TREE (reviewed against js/data/techs.js, 2026-08-10)

Every civ also has 5 shared Level 0 techs, auto-completed for free at civ
creation (never actually researched): Pioneer, Distant Horizons (Scout),
Distant Shores (Galley), Hunt Game, Farm Soil. Not listed below since
they're identical for every race.

COST MODEL: every tech's real cost is purely layer-based (doubling each
layer: L1=20 total, L2=40, L3=80, L4=160, L5=320), split across
harvest/coin/lore by column -- Civic leans Harvest (50/20/30), Building
leans Coin (10/60/30), Military splits Coin/Lore evenly (20/40/40), Mystic
leans Lore hardest of all (10/30/60). The numbers below are the REAL costs
a player pays, not the hand-authored `cost`/`costBreakdown` fields in
techs.js (inert leftover data). A city needs at least as many cities as a
tech's layer number to research it.

FOUR COLUMNS, not three (2026-08-10, user-directed): Mystic split off from
Military -- the Druid and every one of its own ability techs (including
its Raptor/Shadowsteed summons) moved there, since a caster's whole kit
was crowding the Military column alongside straight combat techs. The
Living Forest (Awakened Oak) joined it too, same day -- not Druid-summoned
like Raptor/Shadowsteed, but still nature-magic in flavor (requires
Druidism) rather than a straight combat unlock. The Druid and Awakened Oak
themselves are unchanged; only which tab their techs render in changed.

UNIT STATS are no longer written into descriptions below (2026-08-10) --
the tech tree UI now shows each unlocked unit's live stats (Atk/Def/Mov/
Vis/Range/Siege/First Strike/Double Strike/Flying/Carry as applicable)
computed fresh from units.js at render time, folding in whatever that civ
has already researched, so it can never drift out of sync the way a
hand-typed number in a description can.

CORRECTIONS from the previous version of this file (numbers that had
drifted from the actual implementation): "movement when starting on X"
terrain techs were rewritten as flat movement-cost REDUCTIONS a while back
(2026-08-06) -- every entry below reflects that. Whispering Waters is +0.5
lore per river tile, not +0.75. Hunter's Soul grants +1 range and 25%
Double Strike, not +1 attack -- and moved L3 -> L2 (2026-08-10). Silverlight
Steel grants 10% Double Strike and 2% First Strike, not +1 attack/+1% First
Strike. Upon the Wind grants 40% Double Strike, not +2 attack.


--- CIVIC ---

ELF - CIVIC - L1: "Home in the Trees" - Reduces the movement cost of Forest by 0.5. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L1: "Nature Provides' - +0.5 harvest from Forest. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L1: "The Murmuring of Leaves" - +0.5 lore from Forest. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L1: "Architecture of Light and Air" - +2 lore per building constructed in a city (walls don't count). [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L1: "Tending to the Earth" - Any resource gathering activity only has a 2% chance per turn to exhaust the resource, not a 5% chance per normal. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L2: "Whispering Waters" - +0.5 lore per river tile within a city's radius. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L2: "Reverie of Sunset" (requires "Architecture of Light and Air") - +8 lore from any tile with a ruin covered by elven influence. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L2: "Longstrider" - Reduces the movement cost of Plains by 0.5. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L3: "Dancing Upon Azure Waves" (requires "Whispering Waters") - Galleys gain +2 movement and +1 vision. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L3: "Gems of Starlight" - +0.75 coin from Mountains. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L4: "Wind From Distant Treetops" (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves") - All Forest tiles anywhere on the map are always revealed -- no fog of war on Forest at all. [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L5: "Vision Beyond Sight" (requires "Air Beneath, Eyes Above") - All elf units gain +1 vision (Ranger, Blade Dancer, Druid, Raptor, Shadowsteed, Awakened Oak). [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]

ELF - CIVIC - L5: "Cultural Influence" (requires every other Elf tech already researched) - Tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately, skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait. Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]


--- BUILDING ---

ELF - BUILDING - L1: "Aelderwatch" - Unlocks the Treetop Watch building. The city this building is adjacent to gains +2 vision radius. [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

ELF - BUILDING - L2: "Treetop Snipers" (requires "Aelderwatch") - If an enemy unit is within range, there is a 50% chance each turn that each wall within range will attack that enemy unit [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

ELF - BUILDING - L2: "Altar of Ages" (requires "Druidism" and "Reverie of Sunset") - Unlocks the Altar of Ages building. All elf units created in this city gain an extra 25% XP. [16 harvest, 96 coin, 48 lore]

ELF - BUILDING - L3: "Silverleaf Atelier" (requires "Architecture of Light and Air" and "Gems of Starlight") - Unlocks the Silverleaf Atelier building (+10% coin and +10% lore, this city only). [8 harvest, 48 coin, 24 lore]

ELF - BUILDING - L4: "Wellspring Grove" (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves" and "Whispering Waters") - Unlocks the Wellspring Grove building. All elf units, walls, and buildings within this city's radius heal 5% of max HP per turn (minimum 1 HP), regardless of whether they're resting. [32 harvest, 192 coin, 96 lore]


--- MILITARY ---

ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Watching, Hunting" - Unlocks the Ranger, a ranged skirmisher with a chance to strike before its target can react. Granted free at civ creation, same as every other race's basic unit. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Wind and Flashing Steel" - Unlocks the Blade Dancer, a melee duelist with a chance to strike before its target can react. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L1: "Shadowed Hush, Unseen" - Every elf unit may spend its whole turn to become Hidden for 3 turns (voluntarily cancellable early). Cannot activate with an enemy unit on an adjacent tile. Whenever a Hidden unit is revealed for any reason, it is forced visible for at least 1 turn before it can go Hidden again. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L2: "Whirlwind Strike" (requires Wind and Flashing Steel) - As a normal action, the Blade Dancer attacks every enemy unit within 1 tile of itself, each hit dealing 75% damage (as if attacking normally). Each target may counterattack, at 37.5% effectiveness. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L2: "Strike from the Shadows" (requires "Shadowed Hush, Unseen") - A Hidden elf unit gains +50% bonus to attack score. Attacking uses this bonus, then ends Hidden as normal. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L2: "First Frost of Autumn" - Elf unit attacks have a 10% chance to inflict the Frozen condition on the target (0 movement, -25% attack, for a few turns). [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L2: "Hunter's Soul" (requires Watching, Hunting) - Rangers gain +1 range and 25% Double Strike. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Blade Storm" (requires Whirlwind Strike) - Does not replace Whirlwind Strike -- both stay independently usable. As a normal action, the Blade Dancer attacks every enemy unit within 2 tiles of itself, each hit dealing 50% damage (as if attacking normally). Only targets adjacent to the Blade Dancer may counterattack, at 25% effectiveness. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Steely Eyed" - All elf units gain +1 vision (Ranger, Blade Dancer, Druid, Raptor, Shadowsteed, Awakened Oak). [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Sudden Doom" (requires Strike from the Shadows) - Replaces Strike from the Shadows: while Hidden, a unit's bonus to attack score rises to +100% (up from +50%), and it also gains +10% First Strike. Both bonuses end as soon as the unit is no longer Hidden. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Quick as a Shadow" (requires Strike from the Shadows) - A Hidden elf unit can move at full speed, unlike most Hidden units. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L3: "Silverlight Steel" (requires Silverleaf Atelier) - Blade Dancers gain 10% Double Strike and 2% First Strike. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L4: "Sanctuary under Green Boughs" - All elves gain +20% defense when in Forest. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

ELF - MILITARY - L5: "Upon the Wind" (requires "Hunter's Soul" and "Vision Beyond Sight") - All Ranger and Blade Dancer units gain 40% Double Strike. Ranger units gain +1 range while being carried (by a Shadowsteed). [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]


--- MYSTIC ---
(split out of Military, 2026-08-10 -- see the note at the top of this file)

ELF - MYSTIC - L2: "Druidism" (requires "The Murmuring of Leaves and Whispering Waters") - Unlocks the Druid, a utility spellcaster who may also found cities, in addition to the normal Pioneer. [4 harvest, 12 coin, 24 lore]

ELF - MYSTIC - L2: "Air Beneath, Eyes Above" (requires "Druidism") - The Druid may instantly summon a Raptor to assist elven allies, appearing on an open adjacent tile -- no build delay. Player-facing as of 2026-08-10: available as a "Summon Raptor" ring action, not AI-only. One live Raptor per Druid. Cities cannot build Raptor units. [4 harvest, 12 coin, 24 lore]

ELF - MYSTIC - L3: "Nature's Grace" (requires Druidism and Nature Provides) - The Druid may use this action (costs its whole turn, no exhaustion afterward) to restore between 10% and 30% (random) health to an ally unit within its own range (not just adjacent). [8 harvest, 24 coin, 48 lore]

ELF - MYSTIC - L3: "Roots of the World" (requires Druidism) - A Druid may instantly move itself, or an adjacent ally, to any unoccupied tile the civ has ever explored (not just currently visible). This costs the Druid's entire turn (no move/attack after), and it cannot act again until healed to 100% HP. Player-facing as of 2026-08-10: available as a "Teleport Self"/"Teleport <ally>" ring action, not AI-only. [8 harvest, 24 coin, 48 lore]

ELF - MYSTIC - L5: "Shadowsteed" (requires Air Beneath, Eyes Above and Quick as a Shadow) - The Druid may instantly summon a Shadowsteed, a flying horse made of shadow, the same way it summons a Raptor: appearing on an open adjacent tile, no build delay. Player-facing as of 2026-08-10: available as a "Summon Shadowsteed" ring action, not AI-only. One live Shadowsteed per Druid. It cannot carry an Awakened Oak, a Raptor, or a Galley. While carrying another unit, it takes on that unit's range, attack, defense, siege, and First Strike (keeping its own First Strike if higher), then adds its own bonuses on top. Cities cannot build Shadowsteed units directly. [32 harvest, 96 coin, 192 lore]

ELF - MYSTIC - L5: "The Living Forest" (requires "Druidism" and "Sanctuary under Green Boughs") - Unlocks the Awakened Oak, a living, walking tree and the elves' own siege unit -- rare, but meant to be fielded in numbers rather than as a single centerpiece. [32 harvest, 96 coin, 192 lore]
