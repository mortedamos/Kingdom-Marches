HALFELLOW TECH TREE (reviewed against js/data/techs.js, 2026-08-10)

Every civ also has 5 shared Level 0 techs, auto-completed for free at civ
creation (never actually researched): Pioneer, Distant Horizons (Scout),
Distant Shores (Galley), Hunt Game, Farm Soil. Not listed below since
they're identical for every race.

COST MODEL: every tech's real cost is purely layer-based (doubling each
layer: L1=20 total, L2=40, L3=80, L4=160, L5=320), split across
harvest/coin/lore by column -- Civic leans Harvest (50/20/30), Building
leans Coin (10/60/30), Military splits Coin/Lore evenly (20/40/40), Mystic
leans Lore hardest of all (10/30/60). The numbers below are the REAL costs
a player pays, not the hand-authored `cost`/`costBreakdown` fields in
techs.js (inert leftover data). A city needs at least as many cities as a
tech's layer number to research it.

FOUR COLUMNS, not three (2026-08-10, user-directed): Mystic split off from
Military -- the Trouble Maker and its own Riddle Game tech moved there.
Halfellow's Mystic column is deliberately thin (just these two) -- Trouble
Maker is more trickster-rogue than traditional caster, but its two built-in
tricks (Resource Heist, Unlock the Gate) and The Riddle Game's mind-magic
flavor put it closer to Mystic than straight combat.

UNIT STATS are no longer written into descriptions below (2026-08-10) --
the tech tree UI now shows each unlocked/replaced unit's live stats
(Atk/Def/Mov/Vis/Range/Siege/First Strike/Double Strike/Flying/Carry as
applicable) computed fresh from units.js at render time, folding in
whatever that civ has already researched, so it can never drift out of
sync the way a hand-typed number in a description can.

CORRECTIONS from the previous version of this file: Sneaking Around was
narrowed (2026-07-20) to grant Hidden to the Wanderer only, not every
Halfellow unit -- this doc still said "every Halfellow unit." Rouse the
People's base militia-spawn chance is 5% per attack (verified against
combat.js's maybeSpawnMilitia default), not 1%; destroying the structure
rolls again separately at 15% (an ADDITIONAL roll, not a replacement of
the 5% one). Boomerang's "except Militia and Galley" exclusion was
verified accurate against units.js's exemptFromUniversalRangeGrant flag on
both units.


--- CIVIC ---

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L1: Singing Hills - Reduces the movement cost of Hills by 0.5. (Does not stack with Riverfolk on a hill-with-river tile -- only the higher of the two applies.) [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L1: Riverfolk - Reduces the movement cost of River tiles by 0.5. (Does not stack with Singing Hills on a hill-with-river tile -- only the higher of the two applies.) [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L2: Hillside Harvest (requires Singing Hills) - +0.5 harvest from Hills. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L2: The Road Goes Ever On - +1 lore per road tile within a city's radius, up to 8 road tiles per city. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L3: Riverboat Trade (requires Riverfolk) - +0.75 coin per river tile within a city's radius. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L3: Nice Day for Fishing (requires Riverfolk) - +0.5 harvest from Rivers. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L3: Ice Fishing (requires Riverfolk) - +0.35 harvest from Tundra. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L3: Pub Crawl (requires Neighborhood Pub) - +1 coin per building constructed in a city (walls don't count). [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L4: Community Fellowship (requires Neighborhood Pub) - Gain influence in tiles 100% faster (tile fill-in rate x2.00). [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L4: Hearth and Homeland - Heal an extra 10% per turn when resting on a filled-in tile within one of your own cities' borders (not just inside the city itself). [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L4: Envoy - Pioneer and Wanderer may channel for 2 turns on an already-in-radius, unclaimed tile to claim it outright, independent of the normal gradual fill-in rate. [80 harvest, 32 coin, 48 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L5: Family and Friendship (requires Historical Society and Neighborhood Pub) - +1 influence radius in every city. [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]

HALFELLOW - CIVIC - L5: Cultural Influence (requires every other Halfellow tech already researched) - Tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately, skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait. Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]

HALFELLOW - BUILDING - L2: Farmers Market - Unlocks the Farmers Market building (+10% harvest per turn, this city only). [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

HALFELLOW - BUILDING - L3: Neighborhood Pub - Unlocks the Neighborhood Pub building (+10% coin per turn, this city only). [8 harvest, 48 coin, 24 lore]

HALFELLOW - BUILDING - L5: Strategic Reserve (requires Historical Society) - Unlocks the Armory building. A unit gains +50% attack and +50% defense only while its home city (the city that produced it) has an Armory actually built -- units trained in a city without one get nothing, even if another city of yours has one. [32 harvest, 192 coin, 96 lore]


--- MILITARY ---

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L1: Wanderer - Unlocks the Wanderer, the halfellows' basic melee fighter -- may also found a city, in addition to the normal Pioneer. Also reduces the movement cost of Plains by 0.5. Granted free at civ creation, same as every other race's basic melee unit. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L1: Sneaking Around - The Wanderer may spend its whole turn to become Hidden for 3 turns (voluntarily cancellable early). Cannot activate with an enemy unit on an adjacent tile. Whenever a Hidden unit is revealed for any reason, it is forced visible for at least 1 turn before it can go Hidden again. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L1: Keep an Eye Out - Any Halfellow unit may spend its whole turn to go Hidden and hold position, gaining +3 vision radius for as long as it stays there. Ends the same way Hidden normally ends (moving, an enemy walking onto its tile, attacking, ...). [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L2: Pony Patrol (requires The Road Goes Ever On) - Unlocks the Pony Patrol, a fast-moving scout-fighter. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L2: A Knife in the Dark (requires Sneaking Around) - A Hidden Halfellow unit attacks at 175%. Attacking always ends Hidden as normal. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L2: Boomerang - Every Halfellow unit except Militia and Galley gains Ranged 2 (attacks from 2 tiles away and takes no counterattack when doing so) -- a civ-wide floor on effective range, never lowers a unit that already has more. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L3: Courage (requires A Knife in the Dark) - Wanderer gains +2 attack, +1 defense, and 3% First Strike. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L3: High Ground (requires Singing Hills) - While standing on Hills: +50% defense, and +50% counterattack damage, regardless of whether the incoming attack was First Strike or not. [16 harvest, 32 coin, 32 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L4: Undaunted (requires Pony Patrol) - When a Pony Patrol is killed, 25% chance a Wanderer spawns in its place. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L4: Rouse the Militia (requires Historical Society) - Unlocks the Militia, a stronger standing fighter than the Wanderer. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L4: Devoted Companions (requires Community Fellowship) - Halfellow units may carry any other Halfellow unit (-25% movement while carrying). The passenger heals 50% faster and doesn't need the carrier to Rest. If the carrier dies, the passenger drops onto the same tile. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L4: It's Like the Great Stories (requires Historical Society) - All Halfellow units gain an extra +50% XP whenever they earn XP. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L5: Resilient Spirit (requires Family and Friendship) - If the next hit against a Halfellow unit (forward or counter) would kill it, 25% chance to negate all of that damage instead. Triggering forces the unit to Rest next turn, and permanently reduces that same unit's own trigger chance by 15 percentage points (floored at 0%) -- diminishing returns per unit, never resets. Additionally, Halfellow units have a 50% chance to resist being raised from the dead (if killed by an Undead unit, only a 50% chance the Undead civ turns it into a zombie under its control). [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - L5: Rouse the People (requires Hearth and Homeland) - Cities, walls, and buildings gain attack equal to the Militia's (if higher than their current value) and can now counterattack, but only against an attacker standing adjacent (melee range); a Ranged attack takes no counter damage. 5% chance a Militia spawns adjacent to a structure/city that's attacked; if the attack actually destroys it, a further separate 15% chance also applies. [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]

HALFELLOW - MILITARY - (all techs, race-wide effect): completing any Halfellow Military OR Mystic tech raises this civ's effective militarism by +0.03 per tech (capped at 1.0 total, 2026-08-10: now counts Mystic too, since Making Trouble/The Riddle Game are still combat-relevant even after their column moved) -- Halfellow becomes naturally more willing to fight as its kit matures, fighting smarter (hiding, ambushing) rather than harder like the Orcs.


--- MYSTIC ---
(split out of Military, 2026-08-10 -- see the note at the top of this file)

HALFELLOW - MYSTIC - L3: Making Trouble (requires Pub Crawl) - Unlocks the Trouble Maker, a stealthy rogue with two built-in tricks: Resource Heist (steal a targeted enemy unit's accumulated prospecting/delving/fishing stash, resetting their claim to zero, and leaves the victim Befuddled) and Unlock the Gate (disables a targeted wall and every wall adjacent to it -- zeroing their defense and suppressing any special wall defenses -- for 3 rounds). [8 harvest, 24 coin, 48 lore]

HALFELLOW - MYSTIC - L4: The Riddle Game (requires Making Trouble) - Trouble Maker and Wanderer may pose a riddle to an enemy unit at range. A more curious race is more likely to resist and shrug it off; otherwise the target becomes Befuddled for 2 turns: -50% attack, 75% defense, movement capped at 1, 0% First Strike. [16 harvest, 48 coin, 96 lore]
