Civic

1. Singing Hills vs. Riverfolk "doesn't stack" — a tile can be Hills and have a river on it. If a civ somehow researches both (nothing currently stops a civ from researching every tech in its tree eventually), should a unit standing on a hill-with-river get only +1 total (not +2)? I'll implement it as "at most one of these bonuses applies per tile" unless you say otherwise.

1. ANSWER: Should only get +1 total. This should apply to other places and other tech trees where movement bonuses from multiple tile features could stack.


2. The Road Goes Ever On's "maximum 8 per city" — the existing per-road bonus mechanism (unlock_feature_bonus, already used by Human's near-identical "Trade Roads" tech) has no cap today. Should I add a general cap parameter to that shared mechanism (usable by future techs too), or is this specifically a Halfellow-only bespoke cap?

2. ANSWER: Human "trade roads" tech should have the same cap - future resource bonuses like this granted from roads should also cap at 8. (the intent is to prevent a player from abusing this by filling their entire territory with roads).

3. Pub Crawl's "per building constructed" — does "building" mean just the 4 race-specific economic buildings, or does it also count wall segments (a city with 3 walls + 2 buildings  +5 coin, or +2)?

3. ANSWER: just buildings, not walls.

4. Hearth and Homeland's "tile under your own influence" — does this mean any tile with owned status belonging to the civ (the whole territory), not just city tiles? That's a bigger area than the existing city-only healing-rate bonus other races get.

4. ANSWER: this means tiles where influence has been filled in within their city borders.

5. Community Fellowship's "50% faster influence gain" — this maps to the tile fill-in rate (filledOffsets/advanceCityFill, currently gated by industriousness). Multiplicative on top of the existing rate, correct?

5. ANSWER: Correct

Buildings

6. The three "+10% X per turn" buildings (Farmers Market/Neighborhood Pub/Historical Society) are a genuinely new mechanism — no existing building gives a percentage yield bonus (only flat additive yields or influence multipliers). Does the 10% apply to the city's total yield of that resource (post everything else), or just the tile-worked portion?

6. ANSWER: Should be the same as similar buildings in other tech trees, such as human "Marketcraft". The intent is that any tile with influence filled-in grants its resources to the associated city, and the building increases the resources gathered from these tiles by 10%.

7. Strategic Reserve/Armory — is the +33% attack/defense + Ranged 25% civ-wide the moment the tech completes (like every other tech effect), or gated on an Armory actually existing somewhere (which per your first answer isn't how prereqs work, but this reads like the bonus itself, not just a prereq — want to confirm)?

7. ANSWER: Changing my answer here: for all bonuses associated with buildings, the civ needs to have the building constructed to get that bonus. In this case, its not a resource bonus per influenced tile within the city bounds, but a bonus that applies to all halfellow units. does not stack with additional Armory buildings. The entire halfellow civilization only needs one of these buildings to get the bonus to all of their units.  

Military

8. Sneaking Around — is activating it a full turn-consuming action (like Rest), or a free toggle a unit can do alongside moving/acting? And does the "1 turn forced visible" cooldown apply only when hidden ends naturally/voluntarily, or also when the unit gets caught (enemy walks through it, it attacks, splash damage)?

8. ANSWER: full turn consuming action. 1 turn forced visible applies in any condition where the unit was made visible. Additionally, we need to add to the hidden condition: cannot become hidden if an enemy unit is present in an adjacent tile.

9. A Knife in the Dark — is this one effect (166% attack stat whenever hidden) or two stacking effects (166% attack stat while hidden, plus a separate 166% damage multiplier specifically on the attack that breaks hidden)? The wording reads like it could be either.

9. ANSWER: one effect.

10. How sophisticated should AI "sneak attack" planning be for Knife in the Dark? A simple heuristic (hide when idle near a promising target, attack next turn) is very different in scope from real multi-turn ambush planning.

10. ANSWER: more sophisticated, using the militaristic behavior score to decide on a strategy of isolated ambushes or full on multi unit assaults. If the unit being attacked is stronger than the halfellow and nearby allied units, the halfellow civ should be smart enough to decide on using a sneak attack ambush over a direct assault. Should also factor in movement rates (if I become hidden, will the enemy outpace me before I can attack?). Additionally, they should use the hidden condition defensively (enemy is close, i will probably lose this fight, so become hidden). Should also consider laying a trap using multiple units (let one unit remain unhidden as bait to lure in an enemy unit, then ambush the enemy unit with multiple hidden units that were nearby). Halfellow should fight smarter, as opposed to orcs which fight with brute force. Lets give the AI halfellow a small bonus to militarism for every military tech purchased, so that they will naturally become more militaristic as they become more capable. 

11. High Ground's "counter ranged attacks against unit by 25%" — bonus counter-damage specifically when the incoming attack was Ranged, or a 25% reduction/chance against incoming ranged damage? The phrasing could go either way.

11. ANSWER: bonus counter-damage specifically when the incoming attack was Ranged

12. Devoted Companions — can a unit carry any other Halfellow unit type, or only its own exact type (the Wanderer example reads like a restriction, not just an example)? And does the passenger's faster healing require the carrier to explicitly Rest (matching how healing works for everyone else), or is it automatic regardless?

12. ANSWER: once this tech is purchased, halfellow units can carry any other halfellow unit. Carrier does not need to rest for the carried unit to regain hit points - although, like all carried units, the only thing a carried unit can do is nothing or disembark. Halfellow units that are injured and are being carried should favor doing nothing (unless we deem thar a military strategy is aided by their presence - base this decision on agressiveness). 

13. Resilient Spirit — I'd implement this as a plain inline check during combat resolution (no persistent condition needed, similar to the existing Human Wizard Invulnerability but death-gated) — does that match your intent, and does it apply to every Halfellow unit type?

13. ANSWER: yes, applies to every Halfellow unit type.
