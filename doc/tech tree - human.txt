Instantly loses everything it was claiming/generating if cancelled, the Wizard moves off the Ruin, or it dies. HUMAN - CIVIC - L5: Cultural Influence (requires every other Human tech already researched) - True tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately (skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait). Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. A late-game sink for otherwise-idle surplus stockpile. [40 harvest, 40 coin, 40 lore]
