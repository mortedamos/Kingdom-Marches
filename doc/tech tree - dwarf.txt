
=== CIVIC ===

DWARF - CIVIC - L1: Stonecunning - Dwarves read stone like a map: ignores Hills movement penalty and is able to move on Mountain terrain. Dwarves still cannot build cities, buildings, or walls on mountains. 
DWARF - CIVIC - L1: Wealth of the Earth - +0.75 coin from mountains.
DWARF - CIVIC - L1: Imported Goods - +1 harvest per road tile within a city's radius, up to 8 road tiles per city 
DWARF - CIVIC - L2: Quarry (requires Stonecunning) - +0.75 coin from Hills
DWARF - CIVIC - L2: Deep Lore (requires Stonecunning) - +0.75 lore from Mountains
DWARF - CIVIC - L3: The Long Reckoning (requires Chronicle in Stone) - Dwarves do not forgive. If an enemy civ ever destroys one of this civ's cities or buildings (not walls), that civ is permanently marked as a rival: this civ's units gain +25% attack against that specific civ's units, cities, and structures forever after (tracked per-enemy -civ, never expires, does not require the mark-holder to still be alive) 

DWARF - CIVIC - L3: Prospector's Claim - Any dwarven unit on a Gold Vein or Iron Vein may start Prospecting it: a full-turn channeled action (no move/attack, continues automatically each turn until cancelled by the player/AI) that gradually claims the 1-tile radius around itself, exactly like a city fills in its own tiles (counts toward territorial victory like any owned tile), and harvests the terrain/resource yield of every claimed tile in that radius. Yields +3 coin/+1 lore per turn on a Gold Vein, or +1 harvest/+3 coin/+1 lore per turn on an Iron Vein. Instantly loses everything it was claiming/generating if cancelled, the unit moves off the vein, or it dies. Each turn spent actively working the vein also carries a small chance the vein exhausts and is removed from the map permanently.

DWARF - CIVIC - L3: Chronicle in Stone (requires Deep Lore) - +3 lore per building constructed in a city (walls don't count) 

DWARF - CIVIC - L4: Runeforged Tools (requires Runecraft) - Dwarf units gain an extra +25% XP whenever they earn XP.

DWARF - CIVIC - L5: The Deep Mines (requires Prospector's Claim) - Any dwarven unit that has kept Prospecting the same Gold Vein or Iron Vein for 6+ turns (continuing straight through the same channel that triggered Prospector's Claim at 2+ turns) has the payout REPLACED (not stacked): +5 coin/+4 lore per turn on a Gold Vein, or +1 harvest/+6 coin/+2 lore per turn on an Iron Vein, plus +2 defense while it remains there.

DWARF - CIVIC - L5: Council of the Deep (requires Chronicle in Stone AND Meeting of the Clans) - Dwarven influence counts as 1.25 tiles of influence, in terms of map-filled in toward victory condition. Deliberately NOT a +1 radius bonus like Human/Halfellow's capstone -- Dwarves deepen what they hold rather than spreading wider, so their capstone makes existing holds stronger instead of claiming more ground

DWARF - CIVIC - L5: Cultural Influence (requires every other Dwarf tech already researched) - True tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately (skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait). Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. A late-game sink for otherwise-idle surplus stockpile.


=== BUILDING ===

DWARF - BUILDING - L2: Forgecraft - unlocks the Deep Forge building (+10% coin per turn, this city only) 
DWARF - BUILDING - L3: Runecraft - unlocks Runewall building (walls heal 5% per turn.)
DWARF - BUILDING - L4: Meeting of the Clans (requires Imported Goods) - unlocks the Great Hall building (+5% harvest and +5% lore per turn, this city only)
DWARF - BUILDING - L5: Deep Roads Rite (requires The Deep Mines) - unlocks Deep Gate building. A Dwarf unit standing on any Deep Gate may instantly relocate to any other Deep Gate this civ owns (owner's choice of destination) -- the whole turn is spent arriving, no move or attack after. Dwarf-only: no other race can ever use one. implement pathfinding logic where dwarven units will use Deep Gate to get to their destination, if it is the best option - and new cities will priortize Deep Gate above other buildings, if the tech is unlocked.


=== MILITARY ===

DWARF - MILITARY - L1: Foe Hammer - unlocks FoeHammer unit (basic melee unit)

DWARF - MILITARY - L2: Warrior Poets (requires Deep Lore) - unlocks the Metal Singer unit. Heavy metal / norwegian black metal inspired appearance: has an axe that is also an electric guitar. (utility / caster / buff). range 2.

DWARF - MILITARY - L3: Heavy Metal (requires Warrior Poets) - The Metal Singer exudes an aura in a 1-tile radius around itself (including itself), allied units heal 5% of their HP per turn (minimum 1) regardless of whether they're resting, gain +1 defense, and +25% siege. If the Metal Singer has both this and the Power Metal tech, it can switch between them as an action (both are not active at the same time).

DWARF - MILITARY - L3: Power Metal (requires Warrior Poets) - The Metal Singer exudes an aura in a 1-tile radius around itself (including itself), allied units gain +2 attack and +5% first strike. If the Metal Singer has both this and the Heavy Metal tech, it can switch between them as an action (both are not active at the same time).

DWARF - MILITARY - L2: Thunder from Stone - unlocks the Musketeer unit (range 2).

DWARF - MILITARY - L3: Arquebus Engineering (requires Thunder from Stone) - Musketeer unit gains +1 range, +1 attack, and first strike 50%.

DWARF - MILITARY - L3: Shield Wall - Any Dwarf military unit standing adjacent to another Dwarf military unit gains +2 defense. (Implement dwarf combat strategy to stand near other dwarves during a fight)

DWARF - MILITARY - L3: Runeforged Armory (requires Runecraft) - All dwarf units gain +1 defense, +1 attack.

DWARF - MILITARY - L4: Stonebreaker - All dwarf units gain +50% siege.

DWARF - MILITARY - L4: Epic Metal (requires Heavy Metal) - The Metal Singer's active aura extends to a 2-tile radius.

DWARF - MILITARY - L5: Unyielding (requires Chronicle in Stone) - If the next hit against a unit (forward or counter) would kill it, 50% chance to negate all of that damage instead. Triggering has a 50% chance to force the unit to rest the next turn, and permanently reduces that unit's own trigger chance by 15 percentage points (floored at 0%) -- diminishing returns per unit, never resets.


DWARF - MILITARY - L5: Runeforged Titan (requires Stonebreaker AND Runeforged Armory) - unlocks the Runeforged Titan unit. This is a massive stone golem, slow moving, with a very high defense and a high siege score. Like a wall or city, it takes damage from an ordinary attacker as if brushing it off, but a Siege-property attacker cuts through it just as easily. This is a rare unit. This is meant to plod overland slowly, like a growing threat, and is a city crusher. Other dwarven units will walk alongside this unit and escort it to its target city. This unit never explores and rarely deviates, only to defend itself from attackers along its path to an enemy city.
