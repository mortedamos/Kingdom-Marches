UNDEAD TECH TREE (new file, written against js/data/techs.js, 2026-08-07)

No previous version of this file existed -- every other race has one, so
this fills the gap. Undead's tree is genuinely much sparser than the other
five races' and includes real placeholder ("stub") nodes -- see below.

Every civ also has 5 shared Level 0 techs, auto-completed for free at civ
creation (never actually researched): Pioneer, Distant Horizons (Scout),
Distant Shores (Galley), Hunt Game, Farm Soil. Not listed below since
they're identical for every race.

Undead is the ONE race that hasn't been promoted to its own bespoke Civic
column (Dwarf and Elf both were) -- it still runs on the original shared
"civic trunk" fallback techs (Spoken Memory / The Long Telling / Wardstones
/ Bound Lore), which every other race is explicitly excluded from. These
are included below since they ARE Undead's real Civic column right now,
just not written as Undead-flavored techs.

STUBS: three Military nodes (Wight-Binding, Bone Archery, Grave Colossus
Rite) are placeholders -- `stub: true` in the data, `effects: []` (they
research and cost resources like any other tech, but currently grant
NOTHING). Each names a planned unit it'll eventually unlock. Undead's
Military column is otherwise just Grave-Bound Rite (the starting Skeleton)
-- there is no real Undead military progression yet.

COST MODEL: every tech's real cost is purely layer-based (doubling each
layer: L1=20 total, L2=40, L3=80, L4=160, L5=320), split across
harvest/coin/lore by column -- Civic leans Harvest (50/20/30), Building
leans Coin (10/60/30), Military splits Coin/Lore evenly (20/40/40), Mystic
leans Lore hardest of all (10/30/60). A city needs at least as many cities
as a tech's layer number to research it.

FOUR COLUMNS, not three (2026-08-10, user-directed): a Mystic column was
added game-wide for caster/utility units (Wizard, Druid, Metal Singer, Bog
Witch, Trouble Maker) and their own ability techs, split out of Military.
Undead has no Mystic column content at all right now -- no caster unit
exists yet, real or stub -- so this doesn't change anything below; noted
here only so a future reviewer doesn't wonder why Undead was skipped.


--- CIVIC (shared "civic trunk" fallback -- see note above) ---

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L1: Spoken Memory - +5% civic influence bonus. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L1: Mire-Walkers - The dead do not tire in the bog. Reduces the movement cost of Swamp by 0.5. [10 harvest, 4 coin, 6 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L2: The Long Telling (requires Spoken Memory) - +10% civic influence bonus, stacking additively with Spoken Memory's own +5% (+15% total once both are researched). [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L2: Wardstones (requires Spoken Memory) - +1 influence radius in every city. [20 harvest, 8 coin, 12 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L3: Dark Ritual (requires Mire-Walkers) - A unit stationed 2+ turns on a Ruin channels its power, projecting influence in a 1-tile radius. Vanishes the instant the unit moves off the Ruin or dies. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L3: Bound Lore (requires The Long Telling and Wardstones) - +15% civic influence bonus, stacking additively with the other two civic-influence techs above (+30% total once all three are researched). Also sets a `governanceAvailable` flag on the civ -- as of this review, nothing else in the codebase reads that flag: there's no governance-path UI and no mechanical effect from it yet. [40 harvest, 16 coin, 24 lore]

UNDEAD - CIVIC - L5: Cultural Influence (requires every other Undead tech already researched) - Tech-tree capstone. A city may spend several turns and a steep harvest/coin/lore cost to claim influence over one additional tile within its own radius immediately, skipping the normal gradual fill-in wait. Repeatable until every tile in the city's radius is already its own. [160 harvest, 64 coin, 96 lore]


--- BUILDING ---

UNDEAD - BUILDING - L2: Barrow Rite (requires Grave-Bound Rite) - Unlocks the Barrow building. Contested tiles this civ holds still yield 25% (instead of the usual 0% every other race gets from a contested tile). [4 harvest, 24 coin, 12 lore]

UNDEAD - BUILDING - L3: Reliquary Rite (requires Barrow Rite) - Unlocks the Bone Reliquary building (+2 lore per turn). [8 harvest, 48 coin, 24 lore]

UNDEAD - BUILDING - L4: Obelisk Rite (requires Reliquary Rite) - Unlocks the Cursed Obelisk building (x1.20 influence). [16 harvest, 96 coin, 48 lore]

UNDEAD - BUILDING - L5: Necropolis Rite (requires Obelisk Rite) - Unlocks the Necropolis building (+1 influence radius, stronger Raise Dead). [32 harvest, 192 coin, 96 lore]


--- MILITARY ---

UNDEAD - MILITARY - L1: Grave-Bound Rite - Unlocks the Skeleton unit -- binds the first Skeletons to service. [4 harvest, 8 coin, 8 lore]

UNDEAD - MILITARY - L2: Wight-Binding (requires Grave-Bound Rite) - STUB, no current effect. Planned as a fork: unlock the Wight (a spear-tier unit) OR deepen the Skeleton. [8 harvest, 16 coin, 16 lore]

UNDEAD - MILITARY - L4: Bone Archery (requires Wight-Binding) - STUB, no current effect. Planned as a fork: unlock the Bone Archer (an archer-tier unit) OR deepen whichever unit Wight-Binding resolves to. [32 harvest, 64 coin, 64 lore]

UNDEAD - MILITARY - L5: Grave Colossus Rite (requires Bone Archery) - STUB, no current effect. Planned as the ultimate node: unlocks the Grave Colossus (a heavy siege-tier unit) -- Undead field no cavalry. [64 harvest, 128 coin, 128 lore]
