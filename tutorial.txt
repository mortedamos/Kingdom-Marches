# Kingdom Marches Tutorial

## Your Kingdom

You lead one kingdom on a shared map, growing your cities, training units, and researching new technology while rival kingdoms do the same.

## The Basics

Click a unit to select it, then click a tile to move there. Click one of your cities to see what it can build. Use the End Turn button (or press Enter) once you have given orders to everyone you want to act this turn.

## Cities

Found new cities with a Settler unit. Cities produce resources, build units and structures, and research technology for your kingdom.

## Combat

Move a unit next to an enemy and choose Attack to fight. Terrain, unit type, and technology all affect the outcome.

## Knowledge Base

Open the Knowledge menu any time to look up Units, Structures, Terrain, Actions, Conditions, and Stats.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Press T to reopen this tutorial. See the Keyboard Shortcuts window (Interface menu) for the full list.
